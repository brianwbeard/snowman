About Snowman

Snowman is a kid-friendly word guessing game. Pick letters to uncover the hidden word before your snowman melts away.

Correct letters pop into the word. Wrong letters make the snowman melt a little. If he melts completely, you still get one last chance to save him by guessing the whole word.

There are unlimited games, word lengths from 4–10 letters, optional clues, and progress tracking for every unique word you solve.

Privacy

Snowman does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or other personal information.

Your settings and solved-word progress are stored locally in your browser on this device. That information is not sent to a server by this version of the game.

If you clear your browser's site data, use a different browser, or use another device, your local progress may not carry over.

Links to third-party services, such as Venmo, are governed by those services' own privacy policies.
